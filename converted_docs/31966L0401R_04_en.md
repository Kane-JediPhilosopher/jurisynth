# 31966L0401R(04)

**CORRIGENDUM TO: -  Council Directive 66/401/EEC of 14 June 1966 on the marketing of fodder plant seed** *Official Journal L 298 , 01/11/1997 P. 0070*

Corrigendum to Council Directive 66/401/EEC of 14 June 1966 on the marketing of fodder plant seed (Official Journal of the European Communities 125 of 11 July 1966) On page 2302/66, Article 5 should be replaced by the following: 'Article 5 Member States may, as regards the conditions laid down in Annexes I and II, impose additional or more stringent requirements for the certification of seed and also for the examination of commercial seed, which is produced in their own territory.`
