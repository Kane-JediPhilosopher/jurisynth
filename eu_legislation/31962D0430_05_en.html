COUNCIL DECISION

THE COUNCIL,

Having regard to Article 105 (2) of the Treaty establishing the European Economic Community, which sets up a Monetary Committee in order to promote co-ordination of the policies of Member States in the monetary field to the full extent needed for the functioning of the common market;

Having regard to Article 153 of the Treaty, pursuant to which the Council determines the rules governing the committees provided for in the Treaty;

Having obtained the Opinion of the Commission;

HAS DECIDED

to amend as follow the Rules governing the Monetary Committee determined in the Decision of 18 March 1958:

Article 7 (2) is hereby repealed.

Done at Brussels, 2 April 1962.

For the Council

The President

M. COUVE de MURVILLE
